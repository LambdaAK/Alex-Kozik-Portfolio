# Blueprint redesign (Oct 2026)

Supersedes the "lab notebook" direction, which Alex rejected as too close to Claude's look and too far
from the original site's flow.

## Flow (follows the live alexkozik.com)
Top navbar (name · About Education Experience Research Awards Papers Projects · social icons · theme)
→ About hero card (portrait, "Hello, I'm", name, role, two-line bio, Résumé/LinkedIn/Email/GitHub)
→ Education → Experience → Research → Awards & honors → Papers → Projects.
No Skills section (CLAUDE.md: no skills list).

## Style: Blueprint
- Drafting paper: faint 16px/96px grid on `#f4f6f9`, ink-blue accent `#1d4ed8`. Night: navy blueprint `#0b1c30` with light-blue ink.
- IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono. Mono for section numbers, dates and annotations.
- Frames: hairline borders with ink corner marks (`.frame`). Section titles carry a ruler with tick marks.
- Density: roles show one bullet with "+ more"; projects = 4 featured sheets (figure, title block, two numbers, links) + 8 compact cards + link to all.
- Token names are unchanged so demos and project pages follow the theme.
